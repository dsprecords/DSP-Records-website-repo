About DSP Records

Origins — Dyrtie Sound Productions

DSP began in the early 2010s as Dyrtie Sound Productions — an underground promotion and artist management company built from the ground up. It started with monthly shows at Whiskey Creek, a bar and live music venue in Medicine Hat, Alberta.

DSP did not rely on house systems. Sound and lighting were brought in specifically for bass music. Systems were tuned for impact. Lighting was built for atmosphere. Every event was constructed intentionally.

From those early monthly nights came larger bookings, including headliners connected to major festivals at the time. Momentum grew into weekly Saturday events that opened the stage to more than just the core DSP crew. Local DJs were given opportunities to perform, build confidence, and gain experience in front of real crowds.

DSP was not only promoting events — it was operating as a legitimate artist management company. Five DJs were signed under management contracts. Bookings were arranged outside hometowns. Festival opportunities were secured. Artists traveled. Careers were being built carefully and professionally.

Halloween 2012 marked a defining moment — DJs performing three shows across two cities over two days, each executed cleanly. No chaos. No drama. Just coordination, discipline, and delivery.

DSP’s final nightclub event was New Year’s Eve 2013 at Liquid Nightclub. After that, public promotions and artist management were paused due to life obligations.

If DSP had operated continuously for the last decade, there would be far more to display. But what was built was solid.

The brand never burned out. It stayed smoldering.

Values Forged Through Experience

DSP’s values are not theoretical. They were not adopted because they look good on a website.

They were developed over time — through lived experience, through mistakes, through growth, and through choosing to move differently.

The standards upheld today are the result of years of reflection and rebuilding. Not perfection. Progress. Growth is part of the culture.

At the center of DSP is a PLUR-based value system — Peace, Love, Unity, and Respect — not as a slogan, but as a standard.

In a scene that can easily slip into ego, politics, and short-term thinking, DSP exists to lead differently. Artists supporting artists. DJs lifting each other up. Clear communication. Professional execution. Accountability without hostility.

These values don’t end when the music stops. They don’t disappear when the sun comes up. They carry forward — into studios, into partnerships, into everyday life.

From Promotion to Record Label

DSP Records is the next evolution — a structured label platform built on the same foundation that powered the events.

The mission is long-term momentum over hype. Systems over shortcuts. Community over politics.

The goal is not to inflate the past, but to build forward with stronger systems and clearer direction.

Built systems. Long-term momentum. Real connection.

Always fresh. Still dirty.
