About DSP Records

DSP Records began as an underground event and artist management company and has evolved into a record label built on structure, growth, and real connection.

Origins — Dyrtie Sound Productions

DSP began in the early 2010s as Dyrtie Sound Productions, a small but structured underground promotion and artist management company.

It started with monthly shows at Whiskey Creek in Medicine Hat, Alberta — a bar with a dedicated live music room attached to the main space. The venue wasn’t designed specifically for bass-driven electronic events, so DSP approached it differently.

We brought in our own sound. We brought in our own lighting.

Not to inflate the scale — but to build the environment properly. Systems were configured specifically for bass-heavy music, tuned for clarity and weight. Lighting was added intentionally to shape atmosphere and give each event identity.

Load-in, setup, calibration, teardown — everything was handled directly.

None of it was massive. But it was deliberate. And it built momentum.

Growth & Weekly Momentum

As consistency built trust, DSP began booking touring headliners who were appearing on major festival lineups at the time, pairing them with strong local support.

Monthly events evolved into structured weekly Saturday programming, creating stage opportunities not only for the core DSP roster but for a broader circle of local DJs.

The intention was never exclusivity. It was momentum through inclusion.

Artist Management & Operational Discipline

DSP operated as a legitimate artist management company with formal agreements and structured booking efforts. At its height, DSP managed five signed DJs.

Halloween 2012 marked a defining milestone: three coordinated shows across two cities in two days — executed cleanly and professionally.

DSP’s final nightclub production was New Year’s Eve 2013 (December 31, 2012). Shortly after, due to life obligations, DSP stepped away from promotions and management.

There was no collapse. No fallout. Just real life.

The brand went quiet — but it never disappeared.

UK Radio Takeover — Staying Connected

During the COVID lockdown years, DSP organized a Canadian takeover of a UK-based radio station, coordinating DJs across provinces to broadcast live into the UK airwaves.

It wasn’t about exposure. It was about continuity.

Culture & Core Values

DSP is rooted in Peace. Love. Unity. Respect.

PLUR is not meant to disappear when the sun comes up — it should carry into communication, collaboration, and long-term relationships.

DSP’s values are not theoretical. They were not adopted because they look good on a website.

They were developed over time — through lived experience, through growth, and through choosing to move differently.

The standards upheld today are the result of years of reflection and rebuilding. Not perfection. Progress.

Growth is part of the culture.

From Events to Record Label

After years of live event production and artist management, DSP shifted toward recorded music and structured digital releases.

DSP Records is the evolution of that foundation.

The same discipline that went into production, coordination, and execution now goes into release planning, branding, and long-term artist development.

Where DSP Is Now

DSP was publicly inactive for many years due to life obligations. If it had operated continuously, there would be more to display.

But what was built was solid.

The brand never burned out. It stayed smoldering.

Now it returns with clearer direction and stronger systems.

Because raves don’t end at night. They end when the sun comes up.

Built systems.
Long-term momentum.
Real connection.

Always fresh. Still dirty.
