UNDERGROUND BASS MUSIC

DSP Records is an independent, international underground bass music label based in British Columbia, Canada. Founded in 2010, we focus on bass-heavy electronic music, professional exposure, credibility, and long-term, artist-first development.

Mission & Core Values

Mission Statement

DSP Records exists to build long-term momentum for underground bass artists — through consistent releases, real-world credibility, and an artist-first approach that prioritizes craft, community, and culture over hype.

Core Values

  • Artist-first development: long-term growth over short-term spikes.
  • Quality & consistency: disciplined output, clean branding, and strong delivery.
  • Underground culture: respect the scene, the dancefloor, and the people in it.
  • Integrity: clear communication, fair collaboration, and professional standards.
  • Momentum: success is built — release by release, show by show.
